# v17 literature choice — 2026-09-23

Primary source: Li, Li & Hutter, *Scaling Rough Terrain Locomotion with Automatic Curriculum Reinforcement Learning* (arXiv:2601.17428v1), §III-B Eq.5–7, https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17428v1 . It defines signed change of per-task mean episodic reward between curriculum stages and softmax of the latest learning progress. The paper's experiments use ANYmal D across terrain/velocity task spaces. Its tested rough-terrain categories do not include these Ant stepping-stone gaps, so it is a mechanism hypothesis, not evidence of this benchmark's improvement.

Our adaptation: task identity is family×level of the fixed course terrain lanes. Both arms resample at each completed training episode; control retains fixed inherited probabilities. Treatment uses signed LP for 30 mesh tasks, while flat mass is fixed for safety/performance retention. Base-weighted softmax, clipped LP/beta5, minimum base mixture.4, window1,024/min4, and initial shortened-episode exclusion are local design choices, not reported paper constants. Evaluate full task performance on new maps, not LP itself. We use RewardManager's already weighted/dt-integrated terminal-inclusive per-env episode sums before its reset, rather than averaged log scalars.

Other verified primary candidates deferred: Mittal et al., *Symmetry Considerations for Learning Task Symmetric Robot Policies*, https://arxiv.org/html/2403.04359v1 (requires audited Ant action/joint/grid reflection and frozen-teacher symmetry); Zhang et al., *Learning Agile Locomotion on Risky Terrains*, https://arxiv.org/html/2311.10484v2 (state-action RND adds reward/predictor and novelty-artifact risks); Cheng et al., *Extreme Parkour with Legged Robots*, https://arxiv.org/html/2309.14341v1 (foot-edge penalty targets safe placement, but paper's no-clearance ablation had slightly higher simulated travel performance). None is silently combined into v17.
